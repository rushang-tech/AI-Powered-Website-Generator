Brief to publish

How VeloSite moves from intent to a reviewable website.

The workflow is designed to reduce blank-page friction while still keeping enough control for real product and brand decisions.

Step 1 Capture the brief

Goal, audience, tone, density, motion, project name, notes, and optional brand images establish direction.

Step 2 Generate routed directions

VeloSite chooses structure, art direction, and layout candidates, then builds multiple variants.

Step 3 Refine inside Studio

Update copy, switch variant, remix styles, apply branding, or continue the conversation with the current state.

Step 4 Publish or export

Share a link to the chosen direction or export a ZIP bundle when the concept is ready to hand off.

What goes in Inputs with real leverage
  • Goal and audience
  • Brand tone and content density
  • Motion level and project naming
  • Extra notes, constraints, and references
  • Logos or brand imagery for the generated direction
What comes out A project you can actually review
  • Multiple website directions instead of a single brittle draft
  • Studio-ready preview frame with editable sections
  • Conversation history and saved workspace state
  • Public publish link and exportable ZIP bundle
  • Structured metadata around variants and generation state

Why this is better

Every step is about shortening the distance between idea and usable website direction.

Less blank-page anxiety

A guided brief helps teams start with structure instead of endlessly rewriting the prompt.

More decision clarity

Variant review makes tradeoffs visible early instead of hiding them behind one generated answer.

Faster polishing

Studio controls keep brand edits, copy changes, and publishing close to the preview itself.
