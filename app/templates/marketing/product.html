How it works

From intent to a reviewable website.

Reduce blank-page friction while keeping control for real product and brand decisions.

Step 1

Capture the brief

Goal, audience, tone, density, motion, project name, notes, and brand images.

Step 2

Generate directions

VeloSite chooses structure, art direction, and layout candidates, then builds variants.

Step 3

Refine in Studio

Update copy, switch variant, remix styles, apply branding, or continue the conversation.

Step 4

Publish or export

Share a link or download a ZIP bundle when the concept is ready.
